    Evidence paths

    Compensation and review
    A duplicate synthetic refund is compensated. The rail verifies its receipt and MandateBound records the same-case review. A reviewer can export and replay the saved evidence.
    Policy refusal
    The response fails the policy gate. The completed bundle preserves the decision while act and prove are skipped.
    Clean settlement
    The synthetic action settles. The bundle preserves the rail outcome and prove is skipped unless a dispute is requested.
    Offline replay
    An exported same-case review can be rechecked with the prepared verifier runtime. Replay reads the evidence and runs no synthetic action.

    Stage reference

    Response

    A fixture or a caller-supplied JSON response enters the testbench.

    The run records recognized input settings. File-backed input carries its byte count and digest.

    Passing fixture ↗

    Policy gate

    Constitutional Agent Testbench evaluates the response. Passing lets orchestration continue to a separately constructed proposal.

    The decide artifact records the policy result. A refusal stops the action.

    Pinned testbench ↗

    Synthetic action

    Consequence Rail runs a refund or inventory action with a recovery path.

    The act artifact records the outcome. A receipt-bearing action can supply a rail bundle for same-case review.

    Pinned rail ↗

    Same-case review

    In rail mode, the stack invokes the rail verifier and passes its result to MandateBound for recording.

    The review binds the action ID and evidence digest. Source truth remains unknown and legal effect remains undetermined.

    Pinned reviewer ↗

    Saved bundle

    The orchestrator publishes one complete case and updates its latest pointer.

    The manifest, report and available stage artifacts live under .out/runs/<run-id>/. Inspect reads the saved case.

    Offline replay

    A prepared verifier runtime rechecks a portable export of the same-case evidence.

    Replay reports whether the recorded bindings, receipt verification and reproduced review agree.
